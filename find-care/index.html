FIND CARE

Start with the kind of help you need.

This preview is a curated care map—not a claim that every provider is verified. Confirm credentials, services, availability, and fit directly before relying on a listing.

ONGOING CARE

Senior-dog veterinary care

For a new pattern, medication review, preventive plan, pain discussion, or a clearer next diagnostic step.

  • Ask who manages after-hours questions.
  • Bring the seven-day change tracker.
  • Confirm what needs same-day attention.
MOVEMENT

Rehabilitation & mobility

For slower rises, stair changes, recovery, home setup, assistive devices, or a movement plan coordinated with your veterinarian.

  • Confirm professional credentials and scope.
  • Ask how progress will be measured.
  • Share current diagnoses and medicines.
URGENT HELP

Emergency care

For breathing trouble, collapse, repeated unproductive retching, inability to urinate, severe bleeding, seizures, or sudden inability to stand.

  • Call the clinic while preparing to travel.
  • Do not wait for an email or directory reply.
  • Follow the clinic’s transport instructions.
COMFORT PLANNING

Hospice & palliative care

For comfort goals, quality-of-life conversations, caregiver planning, home support, and what to expect next.

  • Ask who is available after hours.
  • Clarify medication and crisis plans.
  • Write down family priorities and limits.
HUMAN SUPPORT

Grief & aftercare support

For anticipatory grief, family conversations, credentialed counseling, aftercare coordination, and remembrance decisions without pressure.

  • Confirm training and professional scope.
  • Ask about private, group, or crisis support.
  • Take time with memorial decisions when possible.
