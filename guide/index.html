Know what changed. Know what to do next.

The Complete Senior Dog Care Guide turns everyday concerns into a clear plan: what to notice, what to make easier today, and what your veterinarian needs to know.

✓ Six common problem paths✓ Safe at-home next steps✓ A two-minute daily tracker✓ Call-sooner signs and vet questions

Six changes. One clear way forward.

Start with the change closest to what you see at home.

Movement

Record the first rise, slipping, stairs, pace, and recovery.

Sleep

Separate pacing, pain clues, thirst, toileting, and confusion.

Food & water

Measure what went in, what stayed, and what changed around the bowl.

Bathroom

Track timing, urgency, effort, amount, accidents, and distress.

Comfort & connection

Notice which favorite routines are started, finished, or avoided.

When to call

Know which paired signs need a prompt or urgent conversation.

Make today easier without losing the pattern.

Change one low-risk part of the environment at a time—traction, access, lighting, bowl height, a shorter route—then record what actually helped.

1Describe the exact moment.
2Make one practical change.
3Compare the next ordinary moment.

Bring a useful note—not “something feels off.”

“For 10 days, she has paused after naps, slipped twice on the hallway floor, and stopped using the two kitchen steps.”
  • When the change began and how often it happens
  • Which daily function is harder
  • What helps, what does not, and any new medicine
  • One safe photo or video when available

Call sooner when the whole picture changes.

Trouble breathing, collapse, repeated unproductive retching, inability to urinate, seizure, sudden inability to stand, severe pain, or rapidly worsening weakness needs urgent veterinary help.
