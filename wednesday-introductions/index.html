SAFETY & SCOPE

A thoughtful introduction—not a promise of compatibility.

WoafyPet does not perform background checks and does not provide medical, behavioral, dating, counseling, grief-matching, or crisis services.

  • Adults only; no public profiles or precise home address
  • Owner-only first contact; daylight and public if meeting in person
  • Separate transport, easy exit, and no private-home or private-yard recommendation
  • Either person can decline, block, or report a concern privately
  • No contact sharing until both people independently say yes
