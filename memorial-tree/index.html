BEFORE ANY REQUEST IS ACCEPTED

Seven facts, visible first.

A meaningful story is not enough. The service must make the real planting and stewardship understandable.

01

Planting partner

The organization responsible, with a direct source and current contact details.

02

Location

Country, region, land context, and whether an exact site can be shared.

03

Species

Native or suitable species and why it belongs in that ecology.

04

Timing

Expected planting window and what happens if season or conditions change.

05

Stewardship

Who tends the planting and how loss or replacement is handled.

06

Proof

What confirmation the family receives and what it can truthfully establish.

07

Use of funds

A plain explanation of partner, administration, and program costs before activation.
