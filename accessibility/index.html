Current preview

The preview uses semantic headings, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus, descriptive image text, labeled forms, scalable type, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts.

Known status

This is a working preview rather than a conformance claim. Automated and manual checks will continue before launch, including keyboard, zoom, contrast, screen-reader structure, and form-error review.

Feedback

The support route will become the published accessibility feedback channel after its production notification and response workflow is connected.
