USE THE LIBRARY WELL

Observation is a bridge, not a diagnosis.

The best result is a shorter path from ‘something feels different’ to specific information and the right professional conversation.

  • Choose one change instead of reading everything at once
  • Record ordinary moments; do not provoke a symptom
  • Use source links to understand the editorial basis
  • Call sooner when the urgent boundary matches what you see
