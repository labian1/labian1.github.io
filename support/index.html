Common questions

Where is my Senior Dog Care Guide?

After submitting your email, check the inbox and spam folder for the delivery message. If it does not arrive, contact us below with the same email address.

How do I update my dog’s profile?

Open My profile from the navigation, choose Edit profile, update the details and save.

How does Care Circle use my dog’s information?

Age, breed, owner-shared conditions, medicines and the exact change help focus the lesson. You choose whether those pet details appear with a public question.

How do Wednesday introductions work?

Requests are reviewed each Wednesday. When there is a suitable fit, both owners decide whether to exchange contact details.

What does a memorial-tree request include?

You share your dog’s name and one memory. The tribute supports Friends of Usambara’s community planting work and includes a memorial confirmation.
