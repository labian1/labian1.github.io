Six everyday changes the guide helps you understand

Each section gives you specific observations, low-risk support ideas, and clear call-sooner signs.

Movement

Spot slipping, slower rises and changed recovery.

Sleep

Separate one restless night from a repeating pattern.

Eating

Record amount, interest, chewing and nausea clues.

Water

Measure ordinary drinking without restricting access.

Bathroom

Capture timing, urgency, posture and route difficulty.

Daily life

Name the familiar routine your dog now avoids.

A useful record takes two minutes.

  1. What changed? Name the exact behavior or function.
  2. When did it happen? Add time, place and what happened before.
  3. What happened next? Record duration, recovery and what helped.
  4. What else changed? Add food, water, bathroom, sleep and medicines.

Bring a better first sentence to the appointment.

“For ten days, she has paused after naps, slipped twice on the hallway floor, and now avoids the two kitchen steps.”
