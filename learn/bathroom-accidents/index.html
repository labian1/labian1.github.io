CHAPTER 01

Record the accident without blame

ResultYou will know the time, sequence, and body clues that matter.

Note when it happened, the last bathroom trip, route, urgency, posture, amount, appearance, and whether the dog seemed aware or distressed.

  1. Separate urine, stool, leakage, urgency, and inability to reach the door.
  2. Add water, meals, medicines, sleep, mobility, and recent routine changes.
  3. Do not punish, restrict water, or delay an urgent call to finish a log.

CHAPTER 02

Make the route easier

ResultYou will reduce distance and slipping while the cause is evaluated.

Offer more frequent access and make the familiar route safer without changing every cue.

  1. Add traction, lighting, and a shorter route when possible.
  2. Offer calm, more frequent opportunities at predictable times.
  3. Use washable protection while preserving dignity and normal access.

CHAPTER 03

Know what needs faster care

ResultYou will be ready to report urgency, output, pain, and paired changes.

Bathroom changes can involve mobility, infection, pain, medication, digestion, or other conditions. Let the veterinary team evaluate the cause.

  1. Report frequency, amount, color, straining, pain, thirst, vomiting, and energy.
  2. List medicines and conditions, plus any recent diet or schedule change.
  3. Inability to urinate, repeated straining, collapse, or severe pain is urgent.

CARE CIRCLE DISCUSSION

How do I record accidents without making my dog feel punished?

Stay neutral, make access easier, and record timing, urgency, posture, amount, and paired signs.
