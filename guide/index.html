See the change. Make today easier. Know when to call.

The complete guide for movement, sleep, appetite, bathroom, comfort, and daily-life changes.

What to noticeWhat to do todayWhat to tell your vet

Start with the change you can see.

Movement

Time the first rise. Record slips, stairs, pace, and recovery.

Sleep

Separate pacing, pain clues, thirst, toileting, and confusion.

Food & water

Measure what was offered, eaten, kept down, and paired with thirst.

Bathroom

Track timing, urgency, effort, amount, accidents, and distress.

Daily life

Notice which favorite routines are started, finished, or avoided.

Call sooner

Match the change with the signs that need prompt veterinary help.

Turn one vague worry into a useful plan.

  1. NoticeDescribe one ordinary moment exactly.
  2. SupportChange one low-risk part of the environment.
  3. CompareWatch the next ordinary moment and record what changed.

Give your veterinarian the details that move the conversation forward.

“For 10 days, she has paused after naps, slipped twice in the hallway, and stopped using the two kitchen steps.”
  • When it started and how often it happens
  • Which daily function is harder
  • What helps, what does not, and medicine changes
  • A safe photo or short video when available
