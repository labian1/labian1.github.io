WHAT THE GUIDE HELPS YOU HANDLE

Six changes older dogs often show first.

See what to notice, take one safe action, and know what belongs in your record.

01 · MOVEMENT

Movement & stiffness

You may notice Pausing after rest, slipping feet, shorter walks, or avoiding stairs.

Do today Add traction, shorten the usual route, and record one natural rise without forcing a test.

02 · SLEEP

Restless nights

You may notice Pacing, panting, repeated wake-ups, toileting, or confusion.

Do tonight Keep water and the bathroom route easy to reach. Record the time, first behavior, and what helped.

03 · FOOD

Eating changes

You may notice Leaving food, walking away, dropping food, or chewing differently.

Do today Measure what was offered and eaten. Note swallowing, vomiting, stool, water, energy, and medicine timing.

04 · WATER & BATHROOM

Drinking & bathroom changes

You may notice Extra refills, longer drinks, more urine, accidents, urgency, or changed posture.

Do today Measure one ordinary day without restricting water. Record urine frequency, posture, and output.

05 · DAILY LIFE

Comfort & connection

You may notice Less greeting, play, grooming, exploring, or family time.

Do today Offer one lower-effort familiar routine and note what your dog starts, finishes, or avoids.

06 · URGENT SIGNS

Signs that cannot wait

You may notice Breathing trouble, collapse, repeated unproductive retching, inability to urinate, seizure, sudden inability to stand, or severe distress.

Do now Contact urgent veterinary care. Do not wait for a tracker, lesson, or email.
