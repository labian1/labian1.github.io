PROOF BEFORE PROMISE

Every claim needs a status, a method, and a limit.

We are rebuilding in public without pretending the product is finished. This page is the contract: what we plan to test, what evidence will be shown, and what remains for awareness and better veterinary conversations—not for replacing professional care.

AREACURRENT STATUSWHAT MUST BE SHOWNPUBLIC LIMIT

Bed comfort construction

IN VALIDATION

Final materials, dimensions, pressure/support methodology, durability, cleaning, and fit testing.

No final performance language until production-equivalent testing.

Smart Base mechanics

IN VALIDATION

Load path, sensor mounts, floor contact, sealing, cable design, tolerances, and long-run reliability.

No universal fit or final production geometry.

Routine signals

IN VALIDATION

Repeatability across dogs, beds, floors, positions, and ordinary household conditions.

Trends only; no diagnosis, pain localization, or emergency monitoring.

Care education

EDITORIAL REVIEW

Named sources, review dates, veterinary boundaries, emergency guidance, and correction process.

Education does not replace a veterinarian who knows the animal.

HOW RESULTS WILL APPEAR

Readable evidence, including the inconvenient parts.

A finished proof entry should let a careful reader understand the setup, sample, pass condition, result, limitation, and date.

  1. 01

    Question — What decision is this test meant to support?

  2. 02

    Method — What product version, protocol, conditions, and sample were used?

  3. 03

    Result — What happened, with units and uncertainty where relevant?

  4. 04

    Limit — What does this result not establish?

  5. 05

    Decision — Publish, revise, repeat, narrow, or stop.

THE CLAIM FILTER

If the sentence is stronger than the evidence, the sentence changes.

We will not use “clinically proven,” “medical-grade,” “accurate,” “prevents,” “relieves,” or similar language without product-specific support that matches the exact context.

CLEAR NON-CLAIMS

Technology should reduce uncertainty—not manufacture confidence.

These exclusions stay visible across product, guide, and education pages.

No diagnosis

WoafyPet does not identify a disease, determine a cause, or replace an examination.

No crisis detection

The system is not an emergency service or a promise to recognize dangerous events.

No vital-sign claim

No public heart-rate, breathing-rate, or medical-monitoring promise is part of this preview.

ACCOUNTABLE PROGRESS

Follow decisions, not hype.

Founder updates will explain what passed, what changed, and what remains open.
