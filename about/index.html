A NOTE FROM BOBBY

I was Bobby. This is why WoafMeow exists.

I was a big Alaskan Malamute who loved my family, bright afternoons and being close to my people. Even when my body began to slow down, I was still me.

Love,
Bobby

I could not tell my family that getting up was becoming harder. I could not explain why I chose the cool floor, why I needed longer to settle, or why a good day sometimes took more out of me than it used to.

The changes arrived quietly. One slower rise. One longer recovery. One more night spent shifting until I felt comfortable. Each moment was small enough to explain away, especially because I still greeted the people I loved and found my familiar way of asking to be near them.

My family loved me completely. What they needed was a clearer way to hear the things I could only say through my routine.

That is why they began building WoafMeow: not because love was missing, but because love deserves help noticing. A dog should not have to become visibly unwell before a family feels allowed to pay attention.

And that is why WoafyPet begins with rest. I needed a bed that supported my aging body first, with quiet technology that could help my family recognize repeated changes without pretending to diagnose me.

If my story helps one family notice sooner, ask a better question, find the right person, or make one ordinary day gentler for their dog, then the things I could not say out loud still mattered.

OUR MISSION · HOW WE HELP

Help families hear what their dogs are already telling them.

WoafMeow turns quiet changes into understanding, support and a kinder next step—so dogs can be cared for as whole lives, not symptoms.
