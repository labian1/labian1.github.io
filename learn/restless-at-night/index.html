CHAPTER 01

Build a 7-night wake-up timeline

ResultYou will see whether the same trigger, time, and behavior repeat.

Write the time, first behavior, where your dog went, what happened before bed, and what finally helped them settle.

  1. Separate pacing, panting, vocalizing, thirst, and toileting.
  2. Note meals, medicines, visitors, activity, and room temperature.
  3. Record ordinary nights; do not keep your dog awake to test a theory.

CHAPTER 02

Reduce nighttime friction

ResultYou will know how to make tonight calmer without masking the pattern.

Keep the route predictable and change only one environmental detail at a time.

  1. Keep water and a clear toileting route available.
  2. Use gentle night lighting and traction on the familiar path.
  3. Check noise, temperature, and access to the usual rest place.

CHAPTER 03

Ask the right nighttime questions

ResultYou will bring a concise night pattern and know what needs faster help.

Night changes can have many causes. Share the sequence rather than diagnosing it at home.

  1. Ask whether pain, bathroom urgency, medication timing, senses, or cognitive changes need evaluation.
  2. Bring short clips only when they capture ordinary behavior safely.
  3. Ask which signs mean after-hours care rather than waiting.
