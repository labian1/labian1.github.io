01

Understand common problems

Movement, sleep, appetite, bathroom, sensory, cognitive, and comfort changes.

02

Recognize a meaningful change

Compare timing, frequency, context, safe video, and your dog’s normal baseline.

03

Make today safer

Improve traction, access, rest, routines, and the home environment.

04

Understand solution categories

Veterinary evaluation, rehabilitation, nutrition, dental care, and assistive support.

05

Know when to call

Separate watch-and-record changes from prompt and emergency situations.

06

Leave with a follow-up plan

Ask better questions and define what improvement should look like.
